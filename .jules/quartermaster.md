Godot 4 texture compression anomalies: Uncompressed textures in `environment/` (like `compress/mode=0` for ripples and water droplets) consume disproportionately large amounts of VRAM. Switching to `compress/mode=2` (VRAM compressed) combined with enabling mipmaps (`mipmaps/generate=true`) offers significant VRAM savings without sacrificing runtime visual fidelity for VFX masking textures.
